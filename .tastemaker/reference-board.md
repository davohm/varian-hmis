# Reference board

## Source
- User-supplied conversation screenshots of a login card and an operations shell.
- These are the visual reference for hierarchy, density, and component rhythm, not for colour. `AGENTS.md` sets the Varian HMIS palette.

## Design read
- Surface: authenticated operations product, desktop-first.
- Audience: clinic staff working in dense queues.
- Visitor mode: task-oriented, repeat daily use.
- Visual lane: high-trust clinical systems, calm and information dense.
- Dials: high density, low motion, strict hierarchy, minimal decoration.

## Reference strengths to reuse
- Clear brand mark and one-column sign-in card.
- Strong dark rail with scannable role and system navigation.
- Compact top bar with facility, branch, and live state.
- Section headers with short purpose text and icon-accented task cards.
- Stat cards with one value, one helper, and a quiet icon.
- Recent activity as a readable event list.

## Anti-references to avoid
- Gradients, coloured glows, or decorative hero motion.
- Large rounded cards with heavy shadows.
- Text-only feature walls without visual hierarchy.
- Fake metrics or invented clinical records.
